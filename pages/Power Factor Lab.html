Why is reactive power needed?

P → one way, does work Q ⇄ back and forth, builds field
  • Motors, transformers, chokes need a magnetic field. Q builds it.
  • Q does no net work: stored in the coil, returned every half cycle.
  • Q holds up voltage. Grid operators control voltage with Q.
  • Too little Q → low voltage, motors stall. Too much Q → overvoltage.
ΔV ≈ (P·R + Q·X) / V

So: Q is needed — but supply it locally (capacitor / inverter), not from the far-away grid.
