BLDC Ceiling Fan

Brushless DC Technology — Theory & Operation

What is a BLDC Ceiling Fan?

A Brushless DC (BLDC) ceiling fan uses a permanent-magnet motor with electronic commutation instead of mechanical brushes. The rotation speed is precisely controlled by an electronic driver, offering significantly lower power consumption and longer life.

Why BLDC?

  • Conventional fans: 70–80 W  |  BLDC fans: 28–35 W — up to 65% savings.
  • No brushes → no friction, no sparks, no wear particles.
  • Electronic PWM speed control — no lossy resistors.
  • Life: 25,000–40,000 hours vs 10,000–15,000 hours for induction.

Construction of BLDC Fan Motor

1. Stator (Fixed)

  • Silicon-steel laminations with 3-phase windings (U, V, W) in slots.
  • Star (Y) connected; acts as the electromagnet energised sequentially.

2. Rotor (Rotating)

  • Carries Neodymium Iron Boron (NdFeB) permanent magnets — no windings, no brushes.
  • Fan blades are mounted on or coupled to the rotor.

3. Hall Sensors

  • 3 sensors (H1, H2, H3) at 120° intervals detect rotor N/S pole position.
  • Provide digital feedback to the MCU for correct commutation timing.

4. Electronic Driver

  • MCU + 6-MOSFET bridge + PWM generator. Speed varied by duty cycle.

Working Principle

Magnetic attraction/repulsion between stator electromagnets and rotor permanent magnets. An electronic controller sequences stator current using Hall sensor feedback — no brushes needed.

Step-by-Step

  1. AC mains → Rectifier + Filter → ≈300 V DC bus.
  2. MCU reads 3-bit Hall code (H1 H2 H3).
  3. MCU fires the correct MOSFET pair from the 6-step table.
  4. Current through two stator phases → rotating magnetic field.
  5. Rotor PM aligns → rotor turns → Hall detects new position → next step.
  6. Speed: adjust PWM duty cycle → adjust average voltage → adjust RPM.

PWM Speed Control

Speed ∝ V_avg = V_DC × Duty Cycle (%) High duty cycle → High speed Low duty cycle → Low speed (no resistor waste!)

MOSFET Inverter & PWM

Gate Driver IC

  • Level-shifted gate signals for high-side MOSFETs.
  • Dead-time insertion prevents shoot-through.
  • Over-current, over-temp, UVLO protection built-in.

PWM Speed Control

High duty cycle (90%) → High V_avg → High RPM → More airflow Low duty cycle (20%) → Low V_avg → Low RPM → Less airflow

Efficiency stays high at all speeds — no resistive heat waste.
